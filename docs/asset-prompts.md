# Gallery asset prompts

All assets were generated with the built-in image_gen tool for this project. The paintings were exported as WebP from the generated PNGs; the object PNGs retain their generated alpha channels. These are generated fictional works, not photographs of real artworks or people.

## artwork-01.webp
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: original contemporary art gallery painting. An arresting abstract oil painting of overlapping vermilion and coral forms against warm chalky ivory, a small cobalt counterpoint, expressive confident brushwork, refined layered texture and evocative negative space. Museum-quality, sophisticated and distinct. Art fills entire square canvas, no frame, no text, no signature, no watermark.
